# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single owner uses the workbench from desktop and mobile. Desktop is required for local LoRAChef screening; projects, workflow runs, chat, and gallery review must remain usable from a phone.

## Product Purpose

LoRAChef Studio coordinates private character-material generation across Cloudflare, Modal ComfyUI, a dedicated Modal Qwen service, and the outbound-only PC Agent. Success means uploaded workflows can be checked once, reused safely, resumed after the browser closes, and screened without exposing the PC to inbound traffic.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and work-focused. Copy should be direct, operational, and explicit about state, cost, and recovery.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, decorative dashboards, oversized typography, nested cards, unexplained icon controls, and interfaces that conceal GPU, storage, or offline states.

## Design Principles

- Keep the primary task visible and make system state scannable.
- Separate workflow preparation from repeatable workflow execution.
- Make costly or irreversible actions explicit; never switch providers automatically.
- Preserve progress across navigation, browser closure, and Agent disconnection.
- Prefer familiar controls and concise Chinese labels over custom interaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Honor reduced-motion preferences, provide text for status colors and icon actions, and support viewports down to 320 px without clipping or cropped output previews.
